Personal project · In development

Modular Control ESC.

A common motor-drive platform for exploring control algorithms, feedback methods, and rotor-position estimators.

One platform, multiple control approaches

I am designing a modular motor-driver platform to deploy and compare motor-control algorithms and rotor-position estimators on the same hardware. Keeping the inverter and motor consistent provides a common basis for exploring how different control approaches behave.

The planned design uses an STM32H743, a three-phase inverter, gate drivers, current sensing, and DC-link measurement. The power target is 1 kW, with support planned for encoders, Hall sensors, and sensorless position estimation.

Designing for experimentation

The platform is intended to support field-oriented control (FOC), deadbeat control, model predictive control (MPC), and direct torque control (DTC). I am currently working on the hardware that will make those comparisons possible. Control implementation and comparative results will follow as the inverter and measurement interfaces are brought up and tested.
